# Contributor Payments & Bounty Proof

This document responds to community questions (e.g. issue #415) about how
contributor bounty payments are handled in this repository and what proof
of payment is available.

## Status

- Bounty payouts for accepted contributions are made on the Stellar network
  (USDC or native XLM) to the contributor's `G…` account address.
- Payment proof takes the form of an on-chain transaction hash plus the
  Stellar Explorer link for that transaction.

## How to request proof of payment

1. Open or comment on the issue tied to the completed bounty.
2. A maintainer will reply with:
   - the transaction hash,
   - a link to the transaction on a Stellar explorer, and
   - the paid amount and asset.

## Notes for contributors

- Include your Stellar `G…` payout address when claiming a bounty so the
  payment can be settled and verified on-chain.
- Payment records are kept with their corresponding merged pull request;
  ask a maintainer if you need the receipt for a specific contribution.
